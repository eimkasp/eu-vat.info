European Union guidance

Slovakia VAT Guide

Businesses operating in Slovakia must register for VAT if their annual turnover exceeds the national threshold. Foreign companies trading in Slovakia may need to register immediately without a threshold. The standard VAT rate of 23% applies to most goods and services.
