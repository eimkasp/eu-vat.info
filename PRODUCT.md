# Product

## Register

product

## Users

EU VAT Info serves businesses, accountants, tax professionals, developers, and cross-border operators who need dependable VAT answers quickly. They use the product to compare rates, calculate tax, validate VAT numbers, review historical changes, and retrieve structured country data without navigating bureaucratic sources.

## Product Purpose

EU VAT Info makes European VAT rates, calculations, validation, and historical data easy to find, understand, and act on. Success means users can reach a trustworthy answer quickly, understand where the data comes from and how current it is, and continue into deeper country guidance or developer resources when needed.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, calm, and efficient. The interface should feel professionally maintained and precise without becoming bureaucratic, intimidating, or visually sterile.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS styling: excessive gradients, oversized decorative cards, vanity metrics, glass effects, and flashy motion. The product should not resemble a promotional startup dashboard or trade clarity for novelty.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with the task.** Put rate lookup, calculation, validation, and comparison ahead of promotional content.
2. **Make data effortless to scan.** Use strong hierarchy, consistent alignment, and restrained emphasis so users can compare values quickly.
3. **Earn trust visibly.** Surface source, freshness, scope, and data limitations near the information they qualify.
4. **Reveal complexity progressively.** Keep common workflows simple while preserving deeper rates, history, API, and compliance detail for users who need it.
5. **Design for real working conditions.** Prioritize responsive layouts, fast interaction, legibility, and predictable behavior over decorative presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA throughout. Maintain sufficient color contrast, complete keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, reduced-motion support, accessible names and states, and touch targets suitable for mobile use. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status or meaning.
